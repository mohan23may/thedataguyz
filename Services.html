Data Advisory

Data readiness audit & roadmap

Source profiling, quality baselining, PII scan and an object and dependency inventory, turned into a scorecard and a prioritised backlog.

Data quality, cleaning & audit

Configurable DQ rule frameworks in SQL your team owns, de-duplication and standardisation, per-layer load audit, daily failure and remediation reporting.

Data & AI governance, privacy & lifecycle

PII classification and masking, RBAC and row-level security, retention policy with automated and cascading disposal, ISM and Essential Eight aligned handling — plus the AI use register and risk tiering the same controls will have to cover once models start reading this data.

Modelling, lineage & metadata

Future-state data models, source-to-target mapping, data dictionaries and business glossaries, documented lineage across ingestion, transformation and reporting.

Proof: MetLife — organisation-wide PII classification framework and enterprise data dictionary. Uniting — future-state models that cut data-source onboarding time by 60%.

PILLAR 03

AI Adoption

AI projects rarely fail on the model. They fail on ungoverned, undocumented, unreconciled data underneath it. We build the foundation first, then one real use case on top of it — with a person signing off before anything is generated.

AI readiness assessment

Quality baseline, metadata coverage, access controls and lineage gaps — an honest answer on whether a use case can succeed before you fund it.

Enterprise knowledge search & RAG

Scattered content ingested and indexed for Snowflake Cortex Search or BigQuery with Gemini, with permissions respected at retrieval rather than bolted on after.

AI agents & agentic workflows

Agents scoped to one job with a bounded tool list — metadata and DQ agents, reconciliation and exception triage, document and claim intake — each with an approval gate and a logged trail of what it touched.

AI governance & responsible AI

An AI register, risk tiering, accountable-owner mapping, disclosure and escalation paths, and the human-control points — written to the practices your regulator and your board are already asking about.

Evaluation, observability & run cost

Golden-set evaluation and a regression record you can take to a board, grounding and refusal checks, prompt and tool-call tracing, drift and cost monitoring, and retrieval, caching and routing tuned so the per-query cost stays defensible.

LLM metadata & data-quality agents

Column-level descriptions and candidate DQ checks generated for newly onboarded tables, proposed by AI and approved by an engineer.

GOVERNED TO THE AUSTRALIAN GUIDANCE, NOT A US TEMPLATE

Six practices, and the artefact that proves each one.

The Commonwealth's Guidance for AI Adoption replaced the Voluntary AI Safety Standard in October 2025 and sets six essential practices. Most AI governance decks in this market still cite the old standard. We map your use cases to the current six, and leave behind the register, the test record and the escalation path — not a policy PDF.

PRACTICE 01 & 04

Decide who is accountable · share essential information

AI register with a named owner and a disclosure line per use case.

PRACTICE 02 & 03

Understand impacts · measure and manage risks

Risk tiering, a privacy and impact view, and the controls attached to each tier.

PRACTICE 05

Test and monitor

Golden-set evaluation, grounding checks and drift and cost monitoring in production.

PRACTICE 06

Maintain human control

Approval gates, bounded agent tool lists, and a kill switch that someone owns.

Aligned alongside the Privacy Act's automated-decision transparency obligations, the National AI Plan's sector-by-sector approach, and ISO/IEC 42001 where certification is the goal. APRA CPS 230 and 234, ASIC and DTA obligations handled where they apply.

Proof: The Smith Family — Zendesk and SharePoint unified in Cortex Search with no custom integration layer. Woolworths Group — LLM agents generating column metadata and automated DQ checks.

Typical entry point: a 4–6 week proof of value.

PRACTICE 06 · CROSS-PILLAR

AI agents & knowledge engines

Two patterns we see constantly. An enterprise with a datamart and a document estate whose assistant still can't find the right clause — that needs the pipeline, the metadata and the retrieval fixed before any agent is worth deploying. And an organisation with a lakehouse and a thousand reports whose executives still wait on an analyst — that needs an agent on top of the BI it already owns.

Knowledge-engine diagnostic

20–30 benchmark questions tested against indexing, document quality, semantic retrieval and version relevance, so the root cause is confirmed rather than assumed.

Pipeline, metadata & custom RAG

OCR and extraction, clause-aware chunking, standardised lifecycle metadata, hybrid keyword-plus-vector search with reranking, and deterministic source citations inside an RBAC boundary.

Insight agents on your BI

Ask, explain, find and monitor — answered from approved metrics and named source reports, so the number an executive quotes can be traced back. No platform rebuild.

Agent safety & evaluation

Permissions enforced at retrieval, a golden-set answer test, clear handling of unsupported questions, and adoption and cost monitoring after launch.

Proof: The Smith Family — Zendesk and SharePoint unified in Cortex Search. Woolworths Group — LLM agents generating column metadata and automated DQ checks.
