Noviq

AI-based data onboarding

For teams onboarding new sources. A retail analytics team must onboard a dozen new supplier and e-commerce feeds each quarter into Fabric to support demand forecasting. Each feed takes two to three weeks of manual profiling, mapping and governance review, so the forecasting model keeps slipping.

BEFORE

Engineers hand-profile each file, guess data types and keys, write mapping docs, then rework after governance finds PII.

WITH NOVIQ

AI discovers schemas, proposes types, partitions and candidate keys, and drafts the mapping and technical docs; the analyst validates in a review session, then pipeline artefacts are generated.

OUTCOME

Feeds land in days, governance is applied up front, and the backlog clears — onboarding effort down by up to 80%.

Noviq Shift

AI-based data migration

For teams moving an estate. A platform team must move a SQL Server estate — hundreds of tables, views, stored procedures and functions — to a cloud database before a licence renewal, with no in-house conversion skills and an auditor asking for proof that logic was not altered.

BEFORE

Objects converted by hand, dependencies discovered late, and reconciliation is a spreadsheet exercise that slips the cutover.

WITH SHIFT

The estate is analysed for objects and dependencies, SQL / ETL logic is converted with compatibility gaps flagged for engineer review, and reconciliation reports are produced at every step.

OUTCOME

Coal LSL pattern: 100% of objects converted, validated and deployed in three months with audit-ready reconciliation.

THE FRAMEWORKS ALONGSIDE

What keeps the data trustworthy once Noviq has landed it.

Noviq gets data in and across. These four frameworks — the rest of our delivery IP — keep it defensible afterwards. Each came out of a delivered engagement and is handed over as code you own.

DQ & Load Audit Framework

A configurable, SQL-native data-quality framework with real-time monitoring, plus a load-audit layer comparing source and target record counts across bronze, silver and gold.

  • Rules in SQL your team reads, owns and extends
  • No additional licence, no proprietary rule language
  • Hundreds of rules inside a single run window

MetLife — 99.9% integrity and auditability

ADDF — disposal & de-identification

A scheduled compliance trigger routes end-of-life data to automated purge or de-identification, with Power BI tracking of every disposed and masked record.

  • Compliance becomes a scheduled job, not an annual scramble
  • Primary and cascading tables handled together
  • Analytics value kept without keeping the PII

The Smith Family — compliant by default

AI-Ready Knowledge Foundation

Parallel ingestion pipelines — Zendesk via ADLS and an external stage, SharePoint via Snowflake Openflow — feeding one governed Cortex Search index.

  • Native platform ingestion — no custom integration layer
  • Permissions respected at retrieval, not bolted on after
  • LLM-generated metadata on onboarded tables

The Smith Family — one search across two systems

Customer Data Activation Layer

The warehouse-side half of a CDP or engagement programme: identity resolution, consent and suppression, and audience pipelines into platforms such as Braze.

  • Audience definitions live in the warehouse, not a marketing tool
  • Every send reconcilable back to the record that triggered it
  • Consent applied before activation, not after

Questions we always get

Is Noviq a product we have to buy?

No. It's delivery IP we bring to an engagement and run inside your environment. There's no licence, no subscription and nothing to renew — you pay for the engagement, and you keep what it produces.

Does our data leave our tenancy?

The engagement is designed to run inside your Australian environment, and we scope exactly what the model sees before anything starts. We'll walk your security team through it in the demo.

What if the AI gets a mapping wrong?

It will, sometimes — which is why nothing is generated before a named person approves it. Compatibility gaps and low-confidence proposals are flagged for engineer review rather than quietly guessed.

How do we try it without a big commitment?

A 45-minute demo runs it against a sample of your own schema. A four-to-six week proof of value then puts it against one real source or estate, with an evaluation record you can take to a board.

See it run on your own schema.

Not a canned dataset — a sample of your own. Forty-five minutes, no fee, and your security team is welcome on the call.
