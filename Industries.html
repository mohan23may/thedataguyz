INDUSTRIES

Augmenting industry practice with data, cloud and AI.

The discipline is the same everywhere: profile, migrate, prove, activate. What changes is the regulator, the source systems and the question the business is actually asking. Seven verticals, the use cases that recur in each, and where we've delivered.

FEATURED VERTICAL

Retail, QSR & consumer brands

Transaction volume is never the hard part. The hard part is one definition of a store, a product, a customer and a day — across POS, e-commerce, loyalty, franchise systems, supplier feeds and a dozen marketing tools that each hold their own version of the truth.

Trading & store performance

  • Basket, menu-item and day-part analytics for QSR
  • Store, region and franchise league tables on one definition
  • Promotion and price-change lift measurement
  • Like-for-like and trading-calendar logic that survives migration

Supply chain & availability

  • Demand forecasting on feature-ready, reconciled data
  • On-shelf availability and waste analysis
  • Supplier feed onboarding at quarterly cadence (Noviq)
  • Inventory and replenishment data products

Customer, loyalty & martech

  • Identity resolution across app, card, POS and e-commerce
  • CDP and CRM data models (Dynamics 365, Salesforce, Zoho)
  • Event and audience pipelines into Braze and similar platforms
  • Consent, suppression and privacy-safe activation

Where retail and QSR programmes break

Marketing buys a CDP, the warehouse team builds a lakehouse, and nobody owns the layer in between — so audiences can't be reconciled back to revenue, and the loyalty team and the finance team quote different numbers for the same week.

What we own

The warehouse-side activation layer: one customer identity, one transaction grain, consent applied before send, and every audience traceable back to the records that produced it. Your martech team keeps the engagement platform; we make the data under it defensible.

Experience: Woolworths Group — retail and supply-chain data engineering at enterprise scale.

THE OTHER SIX

Verticals we work in, and the use cases that recur.

Government & public services

Multi-agency estates with long-lived legacy systems, strict handling standards and a reporting obligation that doesn't pause for the migration.

TYPICAL USE CASES

  • Oracle and on-prem warehouse migration to AWS, Azure or GCP
  • Agency data integration and service-performance dashboards
  • Controlled data lifecycle: retention, disposal, de-identification
  • ISM and ACSC Essential Eight aligned data handling
  • Row-level access so each unit sees only its own records

Experience: Transport for NSW · NSW Health · NDIS · NSW Department of Customer Service

Financial services & insurance

Where “can we trust this number?” is a regulatory question, not a preference — and the answer has to be evidenced, not asserted.

TYPICAL USE CASES

  • Source-to-target reconciliation and load-audit frameworks
  • PII classification, masking and enterprise data dictionary
  • Regulatory reporting logic (ACCC, APRA patterns)
  • Claims, policy and underwriting analytics
  • Legacy billing and core-system data migration

Experience: MetLife · IAG · Bank of America · RESIMAC · Hartford Insurance
